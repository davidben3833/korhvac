Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

KOR HVAC respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through korhvac.com and how we use it.

What we collect

Information you give us: your name, company or facility, phone, email, service address, equipment details, project information, uploaded files and messages you send through forms, the website assistant, WhatsApp or email. We do not ask for payment information on this Site.

How we use it

To respond to you, schedule and perform service, prepare proposals, follow up on your request and keep records of our work. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the Site and deliver messages, such as website hosting, form-to-email delivery, WhatsApp and our email provider. When the website assistant uses artificial intelligence, the text of your chat is processed by an AI provider to generate replies. These providers handle information only to provide their services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep request and project information as long as needed to serve you and for our business records, then delete or archive it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@korhvac.com.

Cookies

The Site does not use advertising cookies. Your browser may store small settings, such as whether you closed the assistant.

Children

The Site is intended for businesses and adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The updated version applies once posted on this page.
