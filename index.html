About KOR HVAC

A commercial mechanical contractor for the buildings people depend on every day: nursing homes, offices, schools and apartment buildings across the Northeast.

KOR HVAC was built by people who have spent their careers in the field. After more than twenty years of working on boilers, chillers and rooftop equipment, we know that good mechanical work comes down to a few simple things: show up, look closely, explain it honestly and stand behind the work.

We work with building owners, facility managers and general contractors across the Northeast, and with many of the region’s nursing homes and care facilities, where we’ve built long relationships by being the team that answers the phone.

Experience on the Job

More than twenty years on rooftops, in mechanical rooms and in boiler rooms. We’ve installed and serviced everything from a single split system to multi-ton chillers and cooling towers. We look at the equipment ourselves and tell you plainly what we find, even when the answer is a repair instead of a replacement.

Everything in Writing

Every job starts with a site visit and a written proposal that spells out scope, equipment, exclusions, schedule and price. When there’s more than one way to solve a problem, we lay out the options side by side so you can decide with the full picture.

We Stay With You

The job isn’t finished when the unit is set. We handle startup, testing and balancing coordination, and closeout paperwork, then stay on as your service team with preventive maintenance and around-the-clock emergency response.

Who We Serve

Healthcare & Senior Living

Nursing homes, rehabilitation centers and medical offices, where indoor temperature is a matter of resident health and a failed unit can’t wait until Monday. We work around residents and staff, plan shutdowns carefully, and know the inspection and documentation these buildings require.

Commercial Buildings

Office buildings, retail, labs, houses of worship and mixed-use properties. We replace aging rooftop units, upgrade controls and keep tenants comfortable, often while the building stays open.

General Contractors

Multifamily & Residential

Apartment buildings, condominiums and high-end residences, from central plant boilers to individual units, with crews that respect the people who live there.

Services

From a single rooftop unit to a full central plant, we install, repair and maintain the mechanical systems that keep your building comfortable, safe and running.

Installation & Replacement

When equipment reaches the end of its life, we replace it with as little disruption as possible. We install rooftop units, chillers, cooling towers, boilers, VRF and split systems, heat pumps and make-up air units, and we manage the whole job: rigging and crane picks, curb adapters, piping, electrical coordination, startup and testing.

Service & Repair

When something stops working, our technicians come prepared to find the real cause, not just treat the symptom. We work on compressors, heat exchangers, fans and motors, controls and refrigerant systems, and after every visit you receive a written report explaining what we found and what we did.

Preventive Maintenance

Regular maintenance is the simplest way to avoid breakdowns. Our preventive maintenance plans are built around your equipment and schedule, and include filter and belt changes, coil cleaning, refrigerant and combustion checks, and seasonal startups, with records kept ready for inspections.

Ductwork & Ventilation

We install and modify sheet metal ductwork, fire and smoke dampers, louvers, diffusers and insulation for new systems, renovations and equipment replacements, so air is delivered where it is needed.

Emergency Service

Equipment doesn’t fail on a schedule. Our team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with priority response for maintenance customers, nursing homes and other buildings that can’t wait.

Rebates & Efficiency Upgrades

Older equipment costs more to run and breaks down more often. We help owners upgrade to high-efficiency systems through utility rebate and incentive programs that can offset a significant part of the cost, and improve indoor air quality with better filtration, ventilation and controls.
