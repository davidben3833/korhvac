Text Message (SMS) Program

Last updated November 2026

KOR HVAC Service Notifications is a text message program from KOR HVAC, a heating, ventilation and air-conditioning service company in New Jersey and New York. Texts are sent from our toll-free number (800) 617-7600 only to customers who ask for them.

What we text

  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • “Technician on the way” updates with a live tracking link
  • Job completion notices
  • Notices that an estimate or invoice is ready, and replies to your service questions

We do not send marketing or promotional texts, and we do not share or sell your number.

How customers opt in

Customers give written consent in one of these ways. The consent box is never pre-checked and is not required to request service.

  1. Website service form at korhvac.com/#schedule — the customer enters their mobile number and checks the box shown below.
  2. KOR HVAC customer portal at korhvac.com/portal — under “Text message updates” the customer enters their mobile number and checks the same consent box.
  3. Signed service agreement or work order that contains the same consent language and a place to write a mobile number.

We record the date, time, source and number of every opt-in, and every opt-out.

Message frequency and cost

Frequency varies with your service activity, typically 1–5 messages per visit. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to stop all texts — you will get one confirmation and nothing more. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP, call (800) 617-7600 or email info@korhvac.com for help. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Sample messages

KOR HVAC: Your service visit is confirmed for Tue 11/24, 8–10 AM. Questions? Call (800) 617-7600. Reply STOP to opt out.
KOR HVAC: Mike is on the way and should arrive in about 25 min. Track: korhvac.com/portal/track.html?t=XXXX. Reply STOP to opt out.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text message opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the provider that delivers our messages. See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
